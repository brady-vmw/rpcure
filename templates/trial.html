Questions to ask the study coordinator

  • Do I qualify, given my gene, age and current vision? Which tests decide that, and who pays for them?
  • Is there a placebo or sham group? If I am in it, can I get the real treatment later?
  • How many visits are there, where, and over how long? Are travel and lodging covered?
  • What are the known risks, and what happens if I have a side effect?
  • Would joining stop me from joining other gene or cell therapy trials later?
  • Will I be told my own results and the overall results?
  • Will anything be billed to me or my insurance?

Real clinical trials almost never charge you to take part. A clinic that asks you to pay for a stem cell or "trial" treatment is a warning sign. Why that matters.
